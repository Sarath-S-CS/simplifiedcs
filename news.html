Podcasts

Darknet DiariesJack Rhysider narrates real-world hacking and breach stories as compelling long-form episodes - the most listened-to show in the genre.
Risky BusinessWeekly news and interviews aimed at practitioners, running since 2007 - built for signal over explainer.
Smashing SecurityGraham Cluley and Carole Theriault cover the same threat landscape with a lighter, more entertaining tone.
CyberWire DailyA tight daily news briefing hosted by Dave Bittner - built for a quick catch-up rather than a deep dive.
Malicious LifeRan Levi's narrative deep-dives into the history behind major cybercrimes, produced by Cybereason.

Newsletters

tl;dr secA weekly curation of the best security engineering and AppSec writing, without the fluff.
SANS NewsBites / @RISK / OUCH!The SANS Institute's trio of newsletters covering news, vulnerabilities, and plain-language awareness tips.
Unsupervised LearningDaniel Miessler's newsletter sitting right at the intersection of security and AI - directly relevant to the trends on the News tab.
CloudSecListMarco Lancini's weekly, low-noise roundup of cloud-native security research and tooling.

Medium

InfoSec Write-upsThe largest cybersecurity publication on Medium - bug bounty write-ups, CTF walkthroughs, and real vulnerability research, with its own weekly digest.

News Sites

BleepingComputerFast, detailed coverage of breaking vulnerabilities, ransomware, and malware - often first to publish technical detail on an active incident.
Krebs on SecurityBrian Krebs' investigative reporting - slower, deeper breach investigations rather than daily news volume.
SecurityWeekBroad daily coverage across vulnerabilities, breaches, and industry news - a solid single feed to check regularly.
